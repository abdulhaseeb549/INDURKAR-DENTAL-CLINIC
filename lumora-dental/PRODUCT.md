## Platform

web

## Users

Families in Nanded, Maharashtra, looking for a trusted, gentle dentist for themselves, their parents and their children. They usually find the clinic on Google or by word of mouth, check reviews and timings on their phone, then call or WhatsApp to book. Many are anxious about dental pain.

## Product Purpose

The website for PreDent Superspeciality Dental Clinic, Nanded. It turns a visitor into a booked visit: by phone, by WhatsApp, or through the callback form (which opens a prefilled WhatsApp to the clinic). Success means more local families contacting the clinic, and the site being something the clinic owner is proud to share.

## Positioning

A specialist-led neighbourhood clinic: run by Dr. Prajakta Rangdal (Ambekar), a periodontist (BDS, MDS), with a 4.8 Google rating across 119 reviews that repeatedly mention painless treatment, clear explanations and gentle care for children. The clinic's own line is "Introducing PEACE & EASE in the world of Dentistry" and its tagline is "Precision In Dentistry".

## Product Principles

1. Calm over clinical: the site should lower anxiety, echoing "peace & ease".
2. Booking is one tap away on every screen, by WhatsApp or phone.
3. Only true claims: every number and promise must be backed by the listings or the clinic.
4. Local and personal: a real doctor in a real Nanded neighbourhood, not a chain.
