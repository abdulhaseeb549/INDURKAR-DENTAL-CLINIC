Before your first visit

Will the treatment hurt?

Most treatments are done under local anaesthesia and feel comfortable. Dr. Prajakta explains each step first, so nothing comes as a surprise, and conscious sedation is available if you are very anxious.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on the treatment you need. We examine you first, explain your options, and share the charges before any treatment begins.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see children and adults, and take extra time to keep young patients calm and comfortable.

What if I have pain or swelling today?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 83904 26787 during clinic hours and we will see you as soon as possible.

How often should I get a check-up?

Every six months is a good habit. Regular check-ups catch small problems before they become painful or costly.
